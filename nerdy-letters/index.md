# Nerdy Letters

A series on reteaching math. The premise: most of us were handed the rules
before we were handed a reason to care about them, and that ordering is
backwards. Each letter picks one concept and rebuilds it from something you
already believe about the world.

Written without outside reference or tools — just the visualization I've come
to describe the world with.
